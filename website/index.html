Standalone launcher page?

Not possible, and we checked: Battle Draw refuses to be embedded in other sites (a frame-ancestors policy) and browsers do not let one site control another, so a launcher page cannot drive the real game. The console snippet and the bookmarklet are the only extension-free ways in.

School or work computers

Nothing here needs admin rights or installs anything, but managed devices may still block it. Honest list of what can go wrong:

  • Developer Tools disabled by policy - the console won't open. Bookmarklets may still work.
  • Pasting into the console / javascript: URLs blocked by policy or a content filter.
  • The network blocks the script host or the multiplayer server - the snippet shows an alert.
  • Battle Draw itself may be blocked (it is a game).

We don't provide ways around such restrictions; if your network blocks it, it is blocked.

How a match goes

  1. Create / join a room (5-letter code, 2-4 players). The host chooses Team battle or Free-for-all, assigns teams (1v1, 2v2, 3v1…) and the preparation time (1, 3, 5, 10 min or unlimited). Everyone presses Ready.
  2. Preparation. Everybody draws their own army in their own copy of the game, inside their own zone. Nobody sees or can touch anyone else's army.
  3. Lock. When time is up (or everyone is done) the armies are collected and shown to everyone.
  4. Battle. Every copy of the game builds the same battle (same armies, same seed) and starts it at the same moment. The host can pause and resume for everybody.
  5. Result. The winner is accepted only if the players' copies of the simulation agree. Then: rematch or back to the lobby.

Use a window of at least ~900 x 620 px (browser zoom out with Ctrl + - on small screens). Water, ice and banana peels are not carried into shared battles; units, walls and mines are.
